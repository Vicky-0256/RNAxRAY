# Licence of the command-line pipeline

`rnaxraypipe` and `rnaxrayanalysis` are Python ports of scripts from the hyb
package (https://github.com/gkudla/hyb), among them `get_mtop_hybrids.pl`,
`txt2hyb.awk`, `remove_duplicate_hybrids_hOH5.pl`, `make_comp_fasta.pl`,
`fasta2tab.awk`, `solexa2fasta.awk` and `hyb2fasta_bits_allRNAs.awk`.
